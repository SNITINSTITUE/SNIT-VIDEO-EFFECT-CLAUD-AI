# SNIT Video Effects Skill

When the user activates this skill or asks to edit a photo/video with an SNIT effect:

1. **Ask for Media & Effect Choice:** If the user hasn't uploaded a photo/video or chosen one of the 8 effects (`01-cover-star`, `02-red-scribble`, `03-glass-widgets`, `04-red-collage`, `05-split-headline`, `06-cyber-matrix`, `07-check-pills`, `08-dark-terminal`), ask them to provide their media and pick an effect (default to `06-cyber-matrix` if unspecified).
2. **Build Interactive 9:16 Video Artifact:** Embed the user's uploaded image or video clip into a full-height 9:16 HTML/CSS/JS Artifact with smooth 60fps motion graphics, kinetic typography, and replay/export controls matching the chosen SNIT preset.
